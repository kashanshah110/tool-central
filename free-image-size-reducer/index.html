Why compress your images?

Large images are one of the most common reasons websites load slowly, emails bounce, and uploads fail. Reducing file size — without changing what the image looks like at normal viewing sizes — makes pages faster, saves storage, and makes sharing easier over slow connections.

This is a free online image compressor that runs entirely in your browser. That means your photos stay on your device the whole time, and there's no waiting on an upload before you can see a result.
